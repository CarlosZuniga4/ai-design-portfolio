Concept

In Consigliere Finder, you are an Italian Mob Boss trying to find a new Consigliere: your second-in-command. You will first choose three possible mob captains to promote from a pool of 7. You will then go through two stages of talking periods with each captain and depending on your conversations, you decide who moves on to the next round. The captain left after the two elimination stages is your new Consigliere.

I want the player to match their values and their idea of a perfect second-in-charge through the mobsters' traits, personalities, characteristics, and communication. I want this to not be a game you can win, but a game where you get what you want: your perfect Consigliere.

What works

Transitions are very good, as well as the chit-chat stages, ending, and opening scene format and style.

What needs revisions

  1. I would like to add some music to the game, so I need to do further research on how to secure music without copyright consequences.
  2. In the candidate selection stages, the formatting of each candidate in the frame is wonky — the cropping needs to be better.
  3. In the candidate selection stages, I need to edit the images of some of the captains so they look more detailed — similar to how they look in the chit-chat stages.
