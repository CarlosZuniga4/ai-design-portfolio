Consigiere Research and Game Proposal

Choose a second-in-command you can trust—or put the family in the hands of a traitor.

Overview

01

Consigiere Finder

You are an Italian mob boss searching for a new consigiere. From a pool of seven captains, you will shortlist three, interview them, cut the field to two, and make one final choice.

Format
Choice-driven simulation
Core action
Read, question, decide
Player goal
Find your perfect #2

Player journey

02

From seven candidates to one

  1. Meet the family

    The opening establishes the vacancy and invites the player to review each captain's name, nickname, weapon, strength, weakness, and biography.

  2. Shortlist three

    Compare all seven mobsters and choose the three whose traits and values best fit your idea of a reliable second-in-command.

  3. Test their loyalty

    Interview each candidate through positive, neutral, and negative dialogue choices, then eliminate one and question the finalists again.

  4. Name your consigiere

    Make the final appointment. Your tone across both interview rounds determines whether the selected captain accepts the role—or turns on you.

Ending 01 · Loyalty

Mostly neutral or positive answers earn the chosen candidate's trust.

Ending 02 · Betrayal

Too many negative answers lead the appointed captain to backstab the boss.

Case study

03

Researching The Ratchelor

The proposal studies Algorat's The Ratchelor, a compact dating simulation built around choosing contestants, dialogue trees, and progressive elimination.

View reference game ↗

Gap 01

Lore accessibility

Players wanted more context about the contestants. Consigiere Finder responds with a dedicated bio view covering each mobster's upbringing, nickname, traits, and history.

Gap 02

Dialogue impact

In the reference, dialogue changes expressions but has little effect on the outcome. Here, repeated disrespect creates a concrete risk: the selected captain can betray you.

Style & sound

04

A hand-drawn, 8-bit crime world

The family is watching.

Visual language

Pixel characters, framed scenes, textured wallpaper, and a self-drawn interface.

Game world

A focused sequence of locations: the hangout, candidate wall, interviews, and ending scene.

Audio direction

Tense game-show energy during selections and dramatic crime storytelling during conversations.

Build plan

05

Two weeks from concept to playable

01

Mobsters

  • Create pixel versions of ten mobsters.
  • Write names, nicknames, hometowns, strengths, weaknesses, and biographies.
  • Prepare dialogue for two interviews and the final appointment.

02

Interface

  • Design wallpaper, scene frames, and supporting textures.
  • Build an on-screen volume control.

03

Environments

  • Build the mob hangout and candidate portrait wall.
  • Create both the acceptance and betrayal scenes.

04

Dialogue system

  • Offer positive, neutral, and negative responses.
  • Randomize one option of each type for every dialogue tree.
