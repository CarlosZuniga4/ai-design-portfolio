My Thoughts After Iteration #1

  1. The Corona bottle should have the Corona Extra label on it. It should also look like a 40 oz, being thicker than the current bottle.
  2. The surfboard should be closer up, up against the driftwood. So it should only be showing ⅔ of the surfboard and the surfboard should be bigger. Keep everything the same other than that.
  3. Even though I asked that the beer and the surfboard be the same distance from the CD player, I like it being offset a little. Width-wise, it seems as though the bottle is 5x closer to the CD player than the surfboard.
  4. I forgot to add the song title and artist, which were a couple of requirements for this assignment, so I told the AI to put the words “Sublime” (the artist) in black and Old English font, covering the top 20% of the picture, with a smaller subheading, “Badfish” (the song name) also in black and Old English, right below it, being 50% of the size of “Sublime.”
