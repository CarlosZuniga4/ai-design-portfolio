Revisions made

01

Revisions made:

  1. There were comments about making the interaction in the chit-chat stage more unique by enhancing character personality and helping players feel the impact of their conversation choices. Because of this, I added facial expressions and gestures to the boss and the captains depending on what response the user selected for the chit-chat stages.
  2. There were issues with the formatting of the characters in the “Choose your captains” stages so I told Codex to remove the white background in the frames, lower the captains so that their heads don’t overlap the frames and ensure there isn’t a gap between the bottom of the captains’ torsos and the bottom of the frame.
  3. I added music to the chit-chat stages to help the ambiance. This music was 8-bit-like Italian restaurant music.
  4. Due to the unique facial expressions and gestures seen after the player selects a specific response in the chit-chat stage, I had to increase the time between the selection of the response and the entrance of the next captain, so the player can see the reaction both characters have after the response they chose.
